Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

YesLaku ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This document outlines our data deletion policy and how you can manage your data.

Account and Data Deletion

We give you full control over your data. If you no longer wish to use YesLaku, you can request the deletion of your account and all associated data.

How to request account and data deletion:
  1. Inside the App: You can delete your account directly from within the YesLaku app by navigating to the Settings/Profile menu and selecting "Delete Account".
  2. Via Email: Alternatively, you can request account deletion by sending an email to amikastudios@gmail.com amikastudio@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address.

What happens when you delete your account?

When you request account deletion, all your personal information (such as email), business data, and financial records associated with your account will be automatically and permanently deleted from our secure servers within 7 days. This action cannot be undone.

Data Collection & Security

YesLaku collects email addresses for login purposes (via Firebase Authentication) and diagnostic data (via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure the app runs smoothly. All data is securely encrypted in transit.
